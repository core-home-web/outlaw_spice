Why Outlaw

Built for the Pit, Not the Pantry

We make rubs for people who light charcoal on a Tuesday. Every blend is designed to stand up to smoke, flame and long cooks, and to build the kind of bark that gets photographed before it gets eaten.

  • Made for live fireCoarse, bark-building grinds that hold up from 225°F smokes to 700°F sears.
