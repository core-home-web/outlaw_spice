Cowboy Coffee Ribeyes

Thick-cut ribeyes with a dark, roasty coffee crust, seared hard over a two-zone fire and finished with herb butter.

Prep
10 min
Cook
15 min
Serves
2
Level
Easy
Ingredients
The blendCowboy Coffee Rub
Shop it →
Method
  1. Pull steaks from the fridge 30–45 minutes before cooking. Pat dry and season heavy on all sides with Cowboy Coffee Rub.
  2. Build a two-zone fire: coals (or burners) on high on one side, nothing on the other. Clean and oil the grate.
  3. Sear steaks over direct heat 2–3 minutes per side until a dark crust forms.
  4. Move to the cool side, close the lid and cook to 125°F internal for medium-rare.
  5. Melt butter with rosemary and garlic in a small pan on the grill. Spoon over the steaks and rest 8 minutes before slicing.
Pitmaster tipCoffee rubs darken fast. If the crust is getting there before the inside is, go indirect sooner.
