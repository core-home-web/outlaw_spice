Kansas City Sticky Ribs

St. Louis-cut spare ribs smoked low, wrapped with butter and honey, then lacquered with sauce until they shine.

Prep
15 min
Cook
5–6 hrs
Serves
4
Level
Medium
Ingredients
The blendKansas City's Best Seasoning
Shop it →
Method
  1. Remove the membrane from the back of each rack. Rub with mustard and season both sides with Kansas City's Best.
  2. Smoke at 250°F for 3 hours, until the bark is set and doesn't smear.
  3. Lay each rack on foil meat-side down over pats of butter and a drizzle of honey. Wrap tight and cook 1½–2 hours more.
  4. Unwrap, brush with sauce and cook another 30 minutes until tacky.
  5. Rest 10 minutes, then slice between the bones.
Pitmaster tipDo the bend test: lift the rack from one end with tongs. When it bends and the bark cracks, they're done.
