Apple & Honey Pork Chops

Thick bone-in chops with an orchard-sweet glaze, grill-marked and juicy right to the bone.

Prep
10 min
Cook
15 min
Serves
4
Level
Easy
Ingredients
The blendApple & Honey Hoedown
Shop it →
Method
  1. Rub chops with oil and season both sides with Apple & Honey Hoedown. Rest 20 minutes.
  2. Grill over medium-high heat 4–5 minutes per side, rotating 45° halfway for cross-hatch marks.
  3. Pull at 140°F internal. Carryover takes them to a juicy 145°F.
  4. Whisk butter with vinegar and brush over the chops. Rest 5 minutes and finish with parsley.
Pitmaster tipBrine the chops for 2 hours in salted water if you've got the time. They'll stay juicy even if you overshoot.
