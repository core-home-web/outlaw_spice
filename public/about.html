Our Story

Grill Like an Outlaw

Cooking isn't just a necessity. It's a declaration of boldness, creativity and a love for flavor.

Why We Exist

Arming Backyard Heroes

Outlaw Spices started with a simple idea: the people who cook over fire deserve seasonings as bold as they are. Our mission is to arm grill enthusiasts, tailgate legends and backyard heroes with the ultimate tools of the trade: blends that turn every cook into something worth talking about.

There's something special about cooking over fire. The sizzle, the smoke and the stories that come with it. Whether you're flipping burgers on the patio or smoking ribs out in the wild, grilling brings people together. We just make sure it tastes like you meant it.

So grab a jar, fire up the grill, and let's cook something unforgettable.

The Outlaw Code

What We Ride For

01

Flavor First

Every blend has to earn its place on the grate. If it doesn't make you go back for seconds, it doesn't ship.

02

Built for Fire

Grinds and balances designed for smoke, flame and long cooks. Not for sitting in a cabinet.

03

Feed Everybody

Gluten free and kosher certified across the line, so nobody at the table gets left out.

04

No Rules

Coffee on brisket. Honey mustard on salmon. Nashville hot on everything. Outlaws cook how they want.
