Cowboy Coffee Rub

Net wt. 11 oz | 311 g

Coffee and beef have been riding together since the trail drives. Cowboy Coffee Rub brings a dark, roasty depth that caramelizes into a near-black crust on ribeyes, tri-tip and brisket. Rich without being bitter — the kind of flavor that makes people ask what you did.

How to Use
  1. Season steaks generously right before they hit a screaming-hot grate.
  2. For brisket, layer it over a base of coarse salt and black pepper.
  3. Rest your meat — the crust sets up while the juices settle.
