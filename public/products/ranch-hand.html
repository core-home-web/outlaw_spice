Ranch Hand Seasoning

Net wt. 11.5 oz | 326 g

Every cookout needs a ranch hand. This savory, herb-packed blend brings cool ranch-style flavor to grilled veggies, chicken, potatoes and corn on the cob. It's also the secret to the best smoked wings dip you've ever had.

How to Use
  1. Toss veggies in oil and a good shake, then grill hot and fast.
  2. Season chicken and let it rest 15 minutes before grilling.
  3. Mix into sour cream for an instant dip.
