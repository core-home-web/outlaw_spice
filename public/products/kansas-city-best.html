Kansas City's Best Seasoning

Net wt. 11 oz | 311 g

Kansas City barbecue is all about the sweet-smoky balance, and this blend nails it. Caramel sweetness up front, a warm, smoky middle and just enough tang to keep things lively. Built for ribs and burnt ends, and right at home on pulled pork and grilled chicken.

How to Use
  1. Apply a thin binder (mustard or oil) and coat ribs on both sides.
  2. Smoke at 250°F until the bark sets and won't smear.
  3. Glaze with your favorite sauce for the last 30 minutes.
