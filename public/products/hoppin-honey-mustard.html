Hoppin' Honey Mustard

Net wt. 11.5 oz | 326 g

Bright, tangy mustard meets mellow honey sweetness in a golden blend that lights up the plate. Hoppin' Honey Mustard is our go-to for grilled chicken breasts, pork tenderloin and white fish — and it makes a mean dipping sauce stirred into mayo.

How to Use
  1. Coat chicken or fish and grill over medium-high heat.
  2. Stir 1 tbsp into ½ cup mayo for an instant dipping sauce.
  3. Sprinkle on roasted potatoes in the last 10 minutes.
