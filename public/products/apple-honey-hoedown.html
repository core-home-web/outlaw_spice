Pork's Best Mate

Apple & Honey Hoedown

Net wt. 12 oz | 340 g

Apple and pork go together like a fiddle and a barn dance. This sweet, mellow rub brings orchard apple and golden honey notes that glaze up beautifully on chops, tenderloin, ribs and poultry. Pair it with apple or cherry wood and invite the whole county.

How to Use
  1. Season chops or tenderloin and let rest 20 minutes.
  2. Cook over medium heat; finish with a brush of butter.
  3. Spritz smoked pork with apple juice to double down.
